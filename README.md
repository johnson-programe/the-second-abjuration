# Netherlands / Dutch Expansion — 尼德兰/荷兰重置

A **Hearts of Iron IV** mod (version **1.19.\***): the Netherlands renounces the monarchy and returns to the **Republic of the United Provinces**.

**Steam Workshop:** https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3828723997
**GitHub:** https://github.com/johnson-programe/the-second-abjuration

## English

- **Republican political route** — Regency vs. Commoners, with a **Balance of Power** bar.
- **Commonwealth Council** joint focus tree shared with the West Indies, East Indies and South Africa (industry / military / expansion / politics).
- **Colonial Obedience** (0–10 per colony, starts at 7): invest to raise it, requisition to lower it — at 0 the colony breaks away.
- **Progressive national spirits** that upgrade step by step as you advance.
- **Reformed military**, Low Countries reclamation (cores), war goals against France and Britain, and **Brother Republics** as a special subject type.

## 中文

- **共和国政治路线**：摄政 vs. 平民，附带**权力平衡**条。
- **联邦议会**联合国策树：与西印度、东印度、南非共享（工业 / 军事 / 扩张 / 政治）。
- **殖民地服从度**（每个殖民地 0–10，初始 7）：投资可提升，征用会降低——降到 0 殖民地会脱离独立。
- **逐级强化的民族精神**：随国策推进不断升级。
- **改革后的军事**、重夺低地（核心）、对法国与英国的战争借口，以及特殊附庸**兄弟共和国**。

## Installation / 安装

1. Put `the_second_abjuration/` and `the_second_abjuration.mod` into your HOI4 `mod/` folder
   (`Documents/Paradox Interactive/Hearts of Iron IV/mod/`).
2. Enable **Netherlands / Dutch Expansion** in the launcher and play as the Netherlands.
3. Target version 1.19.x. Remove older versions (hol_republiek / De_Tweede_Verlatinghe) first to avoid conflicts.
